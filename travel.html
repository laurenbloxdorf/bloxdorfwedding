Travel

Getting to Tuscany

Flying In

Florence Airport (FLR) is closest, about 45 minutes from the venue. Pisa (PSA) and Rome (FCO) are larger, cheaper alternatives with a longer drive.

Where to Stay

We've held a room block at Villa San Michele. Mention "Margaret & Liam" when booking for our group rate.

Getting Around

A rental car is the easiest way to explore. We'll also run a shuttle between the hotel block and the venue on the wedding day.
